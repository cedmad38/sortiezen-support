SortieZen helps families prepare outings with a checklist, a visual schedule and personalised alternatives. The app works on iPhone and iPad with iOS/iPadOS 17 or later.

Start and explore

Choose Français or English on first launch. You can change the language in Settings. “Explore a sample” opens fictional profiles and outings in a separate space. Exit this mode to return to your personal data.

Plan an outing

Add useful profiles, then choose “Plan an outing”. Select the activity and participants, generate the checklist and steps, then edit them. Add your photos, words and a backup plan. Personal text already entered is not automatically translated when you change language.

Child view

Open an outing and choose “Child view”. The arrows and checkmark move through the steps. The lock opens a question to return to parent view. In Settings, you can hide text, change image size or show the next step.

Back up and restore

Settings → Export a backup creates a JSON copy of your data, including photos. Keep it in a private location. Importing replaces existing data after confirmation. Without an independent copy, uninstalling or losing the device may result in data loss.

Assistant and reminders

The Apple assistant is optional and requires iOS/iPadOS 26 or later, a compatible device, Apple Intelligence enabled and an available model. Core features work without AI. Local reminders require a scheduled outing with a time and iOS permission.

Report a problem

Open a support request on GitHub. Include the app version, your device model, iOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. GitHub requests are public: do not attach children's information, photos or personal backups.
