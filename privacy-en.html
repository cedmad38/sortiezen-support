Privacy

SupportPrivacyFrançais

SortieZen By Cedmad stores profiles, outings, photos, preferences and reviews on this device. It uses no account, advertising, in-app purchases, trackers or SortieZen server.

Photos are chosen using Apple's picker. The app does not request general access to your photo library. Imported images are resized and re-encoded in the app's private storage.

The optional assistant uses the local model in Apple's Foundation Models framework when available. It works without an API key or an external service added to the app. You can edit the information before generation. Each suggestion requires your approval before saving.

Optional protection uses Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. SortieZen does not store biometric data. The child-view gate helps prevent accidental changes; it does not protect against malicious access.

Optional notifications stay local and include no name or destination. They require an outing with both date and time. Their display depends on iOS settings and Focus modes.

An exported backup includes profiles, photos, outings and reviews in JSON format, without encryption provided by SortieZen. A PDF includes information from the selected outing. You choose where to save these files and whom to share them with. Protection and synchronisation through Files or iCloud depend on your Apple settings.

Importing a backup replaces local data after confirmation. Reminders and locking are disabled after restoration so they can be configured on this device. Unknown versions, inconsistent files and files larger than 50 MB are rejected.

Deleting a profile removes its photo and needs, and removes its references from outings and templates. Text already entered, checklists and photos added to steps remain in outings: delete them separately if necessary. Erasing all data deletes active data and the app's temporary exports. Copies already exported or backed up by iOS remain under your control.

Without an independent copy, loss, uninstalling or changing devices may result in data loss. Local storage is not an independent backup. Device backups are managed by iOS according to your settings.

SortieZen is a personal organisation tool. It provides no diagnosis, prescriptions or guarantee of preventing crises.
